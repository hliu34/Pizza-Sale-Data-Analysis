# Pizza Sale Data Analysis

## Project Objective
A pizza store owner wants an annual report of their sales for 2015, so that he/she can understand their customers and grow their sales for the following years. 

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/hliu34/Pizza-Sale-Data-Analysis/blob/main/pizza_sales_excel_file.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is total sale from all the pizza orders?
- What is the average amount spent per order?
- How many pizzas were sold?
- How many total number orders were placed?
- Number of pizza per order?
- What is the order volume like for a daily basis?
- What is the order volume like for a monthly basis?
- Which pizza category is contributing the most to the total sale?
- Which pizza size is most popular?
- Top five best selling by revenue, quanitiy, and total orders?
- Bottom five worst selling by revenue, quanitiy, and total orders?

## Project Insight
- On a daily basis, the highest volume of order is on Fridays. Weekends like Saturday also has a large amount of total orders.
- January and July have the highest order volumes
- Classic pizzas contributes the most to the sale, making up 26.91% of the total sales
- Large pizzas are the most popular, making up 45.89% of the total sale
- Top 5 pizzas that generates the highest revenue: The Thai Chicken, The Barbecue Chicken, The California Chieken, The Classic Deluxe, and The Spicy Italian Pizzas
- Worst 5 pizzas: The Spinach Pesto, The Mediterranean, The Spinach Supreme, The Green Garden, and The Brie Carre pizza

## Final Conclusion:
- To improve sales, the pizza owner should set Friday as a "pizza day" for each week, where they can feature discounts/deals for the Top 5 selling pizzas. For example, buy one "Thai Chicken pizza" and get 50% off the second one. These deals should be effective for large-sized and top 5 selling pizzas.
